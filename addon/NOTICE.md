# AzerothVeil — notices

AzerothVeil is a modified version of **PerfectMap 1.5.6** by **sokenn_** (CurseForge), licensed under the
GNU GPL v3 (LICENSE.md). AzerothVeil is distributed under the same license.

- `Engine/` holds PerfectMap's Lua code and `Engine/Data/` its data, adapted for AzerothVeil (renamed
  addon, frames and paths; settings stored in `AzerothVeilDB`; the classic look only; the friends & guild
  feature driven by a module). Every change is marked "AzerothVeil" in the code.
- `Media/` and `Tiles/` are PerfectMap's, unchanged.
- `Core/`, `Modules/`, `UI/` and `Locales/` are AzerothVeil's own (GPL v3).

PerfectMap's own notice, unchanged, follows. Its paths are PerfectMap's (`Tint.lua` is now
`Engine/Tint.lua`, `Data/...` is `Engine/Data/...`).

# Third-party material

PerfectMap is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 (LICENSE.md), because it ships data derived from Questie.

- **Data/Townsfolk.lua** is generated by `tools/townsfolk.js` from QuestieDB's WoW Forever store
  (QuestieDB_Forever.toc) and the NPC lists of Questie's townsfolk menu (Modules/QuestieMenu):
  Questie and QuestieDB, https://github.com/Questie, GNU GPL v3.
- **Data/FlightMasters.lua** (where WoW Forever's flight masters stand, to leave out the game's taxi nodes Forever has
  no one at) is generated by the same tool from QuestieDB's WoW Forever store (GNU GPL v3).
- **Data/VanillaQuests.lua** (the quest IDs of original Classic) is copied from Forever Quest Tint by xanastar
  (https://github.com/alexdia25/forever-quest-tint, GNU GPL v3), and **Tint.lua** draws its marks on this map's quests
  as it draws them (its icon gradient, its quest-name sign from its own Media, by its own settings) while it's enabled.
- **Media/Questie/** holds icons copied from Questie's Icons folder (weaponmaster, available, complete, incomplete, slay, loot, object, interact, event, eventquest, reputation, repeatable), so they show without Questie installed (the map's townsfolk, the quest log's kinds of quest).
- **Quests** on the map are drawn with Blizzard's own templates and widgets (POIButtonTemplate,
  QuestPOIFrame blobs, the quest-offer atlases), as its world map does.
- **Map art**: World of Warcraft's own map art (Blizzard Entertainment), from the WoW Forever client.
- **Tiles/** were composited by `tools/build.js` from OpenMap's processed Forever art
  (`Media/Textures/Forever/MapArt`, `MapBackLand`, `MapSeaField`, `MapSeaDark`, `MapSeaLight`, `MapFeather`, `MapZones`):
  OpenMap, https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap, MIT License, (c) 2026 OpenMap contributors.
  Its hand-painted gap fills are by Santiago Reyes (see OpenMap's NOTICE.md).
- **Data/Caves.lua**, **Data/CaveMinimap.lua**, **Media/CaveMask/** and **Media/CaveMend/** (the caves and underground
  places: where Blizzard's own cave maps lie and their masks, the game's minimap pictures of the others, one mended) are
  copied from OpenMap (`Data/Forever/Caves.lua`, `Data/Forever/CaveMinimap.lua`, `MapCave`, `MapCaveMend`, MIT);
  **Caves.lua** shows them as its `Map/Close.lua` does. The cave maps and minimap pictures themselves are the client's.
  The same file's `MapCityMinimap` (Ironforge's and Undercity's minimap inside) is in it too (`FM.CITY_MINIMAP`);
  **Cities.lua** lays them over those cities up close, and lets the cities in the open fade under the minimap's
  terrain, as OpenMap's `Map/Close.lua` does.
- **Data/Reveal.lua** (the explored-area pieces of Zephras Isle's map, so it shows whole from the start) is copied
  from OpenMap's `Data/Forever/Reveal.lua` (MIT), generated there from the game's tables.
- **Data/Fog.lua** (every explored-area piece of each zone map, to know when one is explored whole, and the zones the fog of war covers) is copied from
  OpenMap's `Data/Forever/Reveal.lua` and the zone list of its `Data/Forever/Fog.lua` (MIT), generated there from the
  game's tables; **Media/FogTerritory/** (each zone map's territory, a mask) is copied from its `MapTerritory` (MIT).
  **Fog.lua** draws a zone not explored whole as the game's own map does (its base art and its explored pieces, the
  client's, at run time), through that territory.
- **Modules/Markers.lua** (map markers) follows OpenMap's map pins (`Map/Pins.lua`, MIT): their sharing protocol
  (addon messages under the prefix `OpenMap`, "P1" map, x, y, colour, note), their colours and their code
  (`OMPINS1:`), so the two addons trade markers; the note cleaning and chat link are done as it does them. Written
  anew here; its confirmation window is AzerothVeil's own (UI/Markers.lua).
- **Data/AreaLabels.lua** (each explorable place's area and the middle of its ground, for its name over the minimap's
  terrain up close: **Labels.lua**) is taken from OpenMap's `Data/Forever/Explore.lua` (MIT), generated there from the
  game's tables and terrain; the names themselves are the client's. Its `FM.AREA_LABEL_ALIAS` (WoW Forever's redrawn
  zones to their old art) was matched on the pieces of OpenMap's `Data/Forever/Reveal.lua` (MIT). Its
  `FM.CITY_DISTRICTS` places the capitals' districts at the middle of their townsfolk in `Data/Townsfolk.lua`
  (from Questie / QuestieDB, GPL v3).
- **Pins.lua**'s townsfolk shown only where you've explored (the Map module's "Townsfolk of unexplored areas" off)
  follows OpenMap's "Only places you've found" (`Map/Stitch.lua`, MIT): the game's explored areas at the spot or a
  little round it, a capital found once visited or explored under it. Written anew here; no OpenMap code is called.
- **Data/Places.lua** (instance entrances, docks) is copied from OpenMap's `Data/Forever/Places.lua` (MIT).
- **Data/Minimap.lua** (the list of the client's minimap tiles, by file ID) and **Media/MiniLand/** (each
  continent's land, the minimap's mask) are copied from OpenMap (`Data/Forever/Minimap.lua`, `MapMiniLand`, MIT);
  **Terrain.lua** draws them as its `Map/Close.lua` does. The tiles themselves are the client's own. AzerothVeil
  modified the masks: OpenMap's `MapGapMask` (the land no zone covers: Ahn'Qiraj's, Zul'Gurub's...) taken out of
  them, so the minimap doesn't show there.
- **Data/DungeonMaps.lua** (which of the client's pictures make up each dungeon's and raid's floors, by file ID) is copied
  from OpenMap's `Data/Forever/DungeonMaps.lua` (MIT); **Dungeon.lua** shows them as its `Map/Dungeons.lua` does.
- **Friends.lua** follows OpenMap's `Map/Friends.lua` (MIT): its addon-message protocol (prefix `OpenMapLoc`,
  so the two addons see each other) and the way it sends and hears places.
- **Media/CityMask/**, **Media/CityBanner/**, **Media/FeatherRound.tga** (the cities' cuts, their zones'
  name banners for them, the round feather) are copied from OpenMap (`MapCityMask`, `MapBanner`,
  `MapFeatherRound`), and the banners' placement from its `Data/Forever/Banners.lua` (MIT). Modified in AzerothVeil:
  Thunder Bluff's (1456) and Orgrimmar's (1454) cuts drawn in closer round the city (Thunder Bluff's side wings gone),
  their title banners kept as they were.
  The cities' own maps are the client's (C_Map art layers), drawn at run time. **Isles.lua** draws Zephras Isle's own map
  (the client's, at run time) as a map apart, as MapUtils and OpenMap open it.
- **Media/DungeonDrawn/** and **Data/DungeonDrawn.lua**: the maps of WoW Forever's own dungeons (City of Dalaran and its
  Underbelly, The Drowned City and Atal'Byzal, Blackmaw Hold, Alcaz Prison, Excavation Site: Wetlands, Ruins of Lordaeron,
  Shaper's Terrace and the Prismatic Talus, Krol'dok Stronghold, The Hall of Thanes) are drawn by **Santiago Reyes**
  (https://sreyesmaps.com, "Atlas de Azeroth: Forever") and used with his permission. Thank you, Santiago!
  Generated by `tools/drawnmaps.js` from the copies MapUtils and OpenMap ship; credited on each of these maps in game.
- **Data/DungeonJournal.lua** (each dungeon's bosses: their list, levels, portraits' display IDs and where they stand
  on its maps, the first source the dungeon maps go by) is generated by `tools/journalpins.js` from the DungeonJournal
  addon (ForeverDungeonJournal, by Exehn), which took the places from the CMaNGOS classic database and the client's
  UiMapAssignment, and the levels and display IDs from Wowhead Forever. **Media/DungeonDrawn/dj_ubrs.blp** (its map of
  Upper Blackrock Spire, from Blizzard's art) and **dj_alcaz_2.blp** (Alcaz Prison's second floor, Santiago Reyes's art)
  are its maps, re-encoded. Used with its author's permission. Thank you, Exehn!
- **Data/DungeonSpots.lua** (where bosses stand on those pictures, from the game's journals) is copied from OpenMap's
  `Data/Forever/DungeonBosses.lua` (MIT): used for the bosses MapUtils has no place for.
- **Data/DungeonBosses.lua** (each dungeon's and raid's floors and where its bosses stand, with their names, levels and
  portraits) is generated by `tools/dungeonbosses.js` from MapUtils' `data/*/instances.lua` and `shared/InstancePins`, and
  **Media/dragon_*.tga** are MapUtils' boss dragons with their level ring painted out (`tools/dragons.js`); the boss pins in **Dungeon.lua** follow its D4InstancePins
  (MapUtils by D4KiR, GNU GPL v3).
- **Data/Levels.lua** (WoW Forever's zone levels) is generated from MapUtils' `zonelevels.lua`
  (MapUtils by D4KiR, GNU GPL v3).
- **Data/Vessels.lua** (the boats' and zeppelins' recorded ways and timings) is generated from MapUtils'
  `data/classic/transports.lua` and `data/forever/transports.lua` (merged as its WoW Forever build loads them), and **Vessels.lua** follows its `transports.lua` (live positions, the
  sightings on board, the shared phases over its addon messages). **Media/MapUtils/** holds its pier and
  vessel icons. MapUtils by D4KiR, GNU GPL v3.
- **Media/Questie/barbershop.png** is Questie's barber icon.
- **Icons**: the game's own tracking icons and atlases, the same ones Questie's townsfolk menu and
  EllesmereUI's map travel pins use. With Questie installed, its quest icons are mirrored at run time
  from the HereBeDragons-Pins registry (nothing of Questie's is changed).

  README.md and LICENSE.md; game content stays Blizzard's, its COPYRIGHT.md) and Wowhead's WoW Forever samples, as
  its files say. Only the numbers are taken (boss, item, rate); NeedRoll itself is never read at run time for them.
